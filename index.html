Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information to provide our services. For document conversion, files are processed temporarily and automatically deleted after 1 hour. We don't store your documents long-term.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the services you request. We don't sell or share your data with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (e.g., API providers for document conversion).

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All file transfers are encrypted, and our systems are regularly audited for vulnerabilities.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll notify you of significant changes through our website or email.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@uaecloudstore.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UAE Cloud Store really free to use?

Yes! All our tools are completely free to use. We're supported by ads and affiliate partnerships, but we'll never charge you for access to our core features.

How often is the earning methods database updated?

We update our database weekly with new methods and verify existing ones monthly to ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.

What happens to my documents after conversion?

All files are automatically deleted from our servers after 1 hour. We don't store your documents long-term and never access their contents.

How accurate are the visa requirements?

Our visa data comes from official government sources and is updated regularly. However, we recommend verifying with the embassy before travel as policies can change.
